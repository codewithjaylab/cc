# CLAUDE.md

## Project

Planning repo for an AI-agent agency for SMBs (tentative name: **VendeMás Business** / **Vende Más Studio**). It currently holds research and planning docs under `docs/`: the Codify Business site scrape and analysis, its real pricing, the business plan and a Mexico market study.

## Language

The team is bilingual (Spanish/English). **Write everything in English from now on**:

- New and updated files (docs, code, comments)
- Commit messages, branch names, PR titles and descriptions
- Replies in chat, unless the user explicitly asks for Spanish

Exceptions:
- Keep **source material** in its original language: scraped site text in `docs/scraping/` and quotes from Spanish-language sources.
- Spanish proper names, Mexican legal or tax terms (e.g. CFDI, IMSS, LFPDPPP) and MXN amounts stay as they are. Add a short English gloss where helpful.

The docs written before this rule (`docs/*.md`) are in Spanish. Translate them only when asked, or when substantially editing one.

## Workflow

- Branch from `main`; open PRs against `main`.
